Jet Pack Gym - Assignment 1
Tyler Stow

Project Description:
This project was created using unreal Engine 5 and it contains a simple Jet pack mechanic the player can use to fly around.

The jetpack only activates when the play presses the jump key twice, this way the player still has all their normal movement abilities plus the jetpacks ability to fly.

For some juice, the jetpack has particles and sound when the jetpack is activated, these effects disable when the jetpack deactivates when the player stops pressing the jump key.

Controls for the Game:
W - Move Foward - On ground and in air
S - Move Backward - On ground and in air
A - Move Left - On ground and in air
D - Move Right - On ground and in air
Space - Jump - Activate Jetpack

Directions for Game Use:
Step 1 - The Player must Launch the Game in order to play
Step 2 - The Player can use there WASD Keys to Move around
Step 3 - The Player must press the space key twice to activate the Jetpack
Step 4 - When the Jetpack is Activated, the player must hold down the Space key to fly Upwards
Step 5 - The player can release the Space key to deactivate the Jetpack and start falling 

Engine:
Unreal Engine 5
